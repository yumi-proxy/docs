**To configure YumiProxy in RoxyBrowser, simply follow these steps:**

1. Open the Incogniton dashboard and click the **“Create Profile”** button to enter the profile creation page.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-194.png "Image")
   </Frame>
2. Open the **“Proxy”** tab, select **“Custom Proxy”**, and enter the proxy username and password.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-195.png "Image")
   </Frame>
3. After entering the proxy details, click the **“Check Proxy”** button to verify the connection. Once successful, click **“Confirm”** to save.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-198.png "Image")
   </Frame>
4. Click the **“Launch”** button next to the profile to open a browser window with an independent digital fingerprint and proxy settings.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-199.png "Image")
   </Frame>

After completing the above steps, you can now use the configured proxy to access web pages in Incogniton.
