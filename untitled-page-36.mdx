**To configure YumiProxy in GoLogin, simply follow these steps:**

1. First, download and install GoLogin on your device.
2. Open GoLogin and register or log in to your account.
3. Click **Proxies** in the left sidebar, then click **Add Proxy** on the right to create a new proxy configuration.
4. Enter the proxy parameters obtained from YumiProxy. GoLogin will automatically validate the proxy—if the connection is successful, the corresponding country's flag will appear. Finally, click **Add Proxy** to save.
5. Once the configuration is complete, you have successfully added YumiProxy to GoLogin.

After completing the above steps, you can now use YumiProxy's proxy to access web pages in GoLogin.
