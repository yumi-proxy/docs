---
title: "Safari Browser Proxy Integration Guide "
---

**Before integrating the proxy, please ensure that the proxy service has been properly configured according to the established settings and is in normal working order.**

The specific steps are as follows:

1. Open Safari, click on **Safari** in the menu bar, and select **Preferences**.

2. In the Preferences window, switch to the **Advanced** tab, locate the **Proxy** option at the bottom of the page, and click **Change Settings**.

3. The system will display the network proxy configuration window. Based on the parameters provided by your administrator or proxy service provider, fill in the proxy server address, port number, protocol type (usually HTTP or HTTPS, depending on your proxy details), as well as the username and password required for authentication.

4. After confirming that all information is correct, click **OK** to save the configuration and close the window.

Upon completing the above steps, Safari will be successfully integrated with the proxy, providing enhanced security and flexibility for your network connection.
