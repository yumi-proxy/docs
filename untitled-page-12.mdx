title: "Chrome Proxy Integration Guide "
---

**Before integrating the proxy into Chrome, please ensure that the proxy service itself has been properly configured and is functioning correctly. The specific steps are as follows:**

1. Install a proxy extension in Chrome, such as SwitchyOmega, or choose any other commonly used extension. Search for it in the Chrome Web Store and add it to your browser.

2. Download the required Chrome extension package, decompress it, and locate the extension folder.

3. Click the three dots (menu button) on the right side of the Chrome address bar, select **Extensions**, and then click **Manage Extensions**.
