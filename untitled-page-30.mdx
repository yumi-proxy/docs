**To configure YumiProxy in BitBrowser, simply follow these steps:**

1. First, download and install BitBrowser on your device.
2. Open it and register or log in to your account.
3. Go to the **Browser Profiles** section and click the **\+ Add** button to add a new profile.
4. In the **Basic** section, specify your profile name and any other information if needed.
5. Next, scroll down a bit. In the **Proxy** section, fill in all necessary information for the proxy connection, including username and password.
6. After filling in all proxy connection details, you can click the **Check Proxy** button to verify if the proxy is working properly. As a lookup channel, we recommend using IP2Location.
7. To launch the profile you created, simply click the **Open** button.

That's it! Now you can use BitBrowser for your purposes, select your desired connection location, and enhance your anonymity and security with YumiProxy.
