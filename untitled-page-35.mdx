**Before configuring XLogin with a proxy, please ensure that your proxy settings are correct to guarantee proper operation.**

1. Open XLogin Browser and click **\[New Browser Profile\]**.
2. In the **\[Setting proxy server\]** window, enable **\[Enable proxy server\]**, select the proxy type, and enter the host and port obtained from YumiProxy. If you choose username/password authentication, fill in those credentials as well.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-231.png "Image")
   </Frame>
3. Click **\[Test Proxy\]** to verify the connection, then click **\[Save\]** upon success
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-230.png "Image")
   </Frame>

After completing these steps, you can now use the YumiProxy service to access web pages in XLogin.
