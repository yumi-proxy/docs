**Before configuring a proxy in Multilogin, please ensure that your proxy settings are correct to guarantee proper operation.**

1. Open Multilogin Browser, click **Proxy Management**, and select **Own Proxy** → **Add Proxy**.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-224.png "Image")
   </Frame>
2. Enter the proxy parameters obtained from YumiProxy, then click **Test Proxy**. The proxy details will be displayed below. After confirming, click **OK**.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-225.png "Image")
   </Frame>
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-226.png "Image")
   </Frame>
3. Click **Environment Management** on the left, and select **New Browser**.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-227.png "Image")
   </Frame>
4. Under proxy type, choose **Own Proxy** and check the proxy you just configured.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-228.png "Image")
   </Frame>

After completing the above steps, you can now use YumiProxy's proxy to access web pages in Multilogin.

**If you have any questions or suggestions during use, please feel free to contact us. We are happy to hear your feedback and provide assistance.**
