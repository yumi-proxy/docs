**To configure YumiProxy in RoxyBrowser, simply follow these steps:**

1. First, download and install RoxyBrowser on your device.
2. Open it and register or log in to your account.
3. Then click the **Create Window** button to add a new profile.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-189.png "Image")
   </Frame>
4. Next, in the **Add Proxy IP** section, fill in all necessary information for the proxy connection.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-190.png "Image")
   </Frame>
5. After filling in all proxy connection details, you can click the **Test Proxy IP** button to check if the proxy is working properly.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-191.png "Image")
   </Frame>
6. The proxy configuration process is now complete. If you need to modify any other settings, make the necessary changes and click the **Create Window** button to save your profile.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-192.png "Image")
   </Frame>
7. To launch the profile you created, simply click the **Open Window** button.
   <Frame>
     ![Image](/images/image-193.png "Image")
   </Frame>

After completing the above steps, you can now use YumiProxy's proxy to access web pages in RoxyBrowser.
