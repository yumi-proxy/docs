title: "VMLogin"
---

**Before configuration, please obtain an IP from YumiProxy first and ensure the proxy settings are correct to guarantee normal operation.**

1. Open VMLogin and enter your account and password (if you don't have one, click **Register New Account**).
2. After successful login, click **New Browser Profile** in the left navigation bar.
3. Add your desired display name on the new browser profile page, and click **Set Proxy Server**.
4. Check **Enable Proxy Server**. Fill in the IP address and port with the IP you just extracted from YumiProxy. Here I used the account secret key extraction, with IP address `207.168.53.119` and port `6930` (you can also use the IP and port obtained through whitelist authentication). Click **Save**.
5. After saving, a pop-up will appear indicating whether the configuration was successful.

After completing the above steps, you can now use YumiProxy's proxy to access web pages in VMLogin.

If you have any questions or suggestions during use, please feel free to contact us. We are happy to hear your ideas and provide assistance.
